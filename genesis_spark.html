Scope of Work

Pre-commissioning cold verification procedures and the system's official first powered sequence initialization.

Installation Checklist

  • Double-check absolute DC polarity on all busbars and terminals using a DMM.
  • Execute main pre-charge resistor sequence for DC bus capacitors.
  • Engage main battery fuses and initialize the hardware BMS core.
  • Power up Cerbo GX first and monitor device discovery logs.
  • Switch on MultiPlus-II main toggle and verify standard standby LED status.
